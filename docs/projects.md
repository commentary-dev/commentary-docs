# Organize work with Projects

Projects organize a shared initiative around objectives, deliverables and reviews. A Project is optional: existing PR, document, Draft, Form, Research and Live Preview reviews remain independently usable. A Tracker is also optional. Linking the same Resource to several Projects preserves its original identity, revisions and app-native threads.

## Create and resume

1. Select your personal or team workspace and open **Projects** in the workspace navigation at `/workspaces/{workspaceId}/projects`.
2. Choose **New project**. The guided form starts with a project brief (title, goal, desired outcomes and optional background), then lets you choose no template or one of seven starting templates: Simple checklist, PM feature initiative, Product research / strategy, Software implementation review, Launch readiness, Architecture review, or Agent-generated walkthrough. Selecting a template fills blank brief fields with starter text; you can edit the brief before creation.
3. For a selected template, optionally include its editable Tracker and example records; this option starts enabled but can be unchecked. Without a template, create a bare Project and link existing work afterward. Planned deliverables are examples, not created review artifacts. Software and Architecture review templates also offer a stakeholder-review ZIP with an overview and six audience pages; download creates no Commentary resources. Template details also offer a portable JSON template download.
4. Choose workspace visibility or participants-only visibility.
5. Use the Project sections **Overview**, **Contents**, **Reviews & guides**, **Evidence**, **Activity**, and **Settings**. Overview leads with the authored goal and desired outcomes; Contents groups linked deliverables and supporting material; Reviews & guides holds individual reviews and review guides. Evidence is a separate searchable collection with an inspector.

Use collection search, lifecycle and visibility filters, and explicit next-page loading. Recently changed orders shared changes; it does not claim a personal visit. Project section, search, selected item, and continuation stay in the URL where applicable. Titles open linked objects; separate options inspect their associations.

## Access and lifecycle

Managers govern participants and lifecycle; contributors maintain authorized work; reviewers read/comment within their existing rights. Bring people into the workspace through Members before adding participation. Agents use durable registered Agent identities and workspace grants; they cannot govern human reviewer eligibility. Workspace membership, Project participation, Resource rights and provider authorization remain separate. An inaccessible linked Resource is omitted, including its title/count/revision; linking grants no source access.

Archive preserves linked Resources and blocks ordinary edits; authorized managers can restore. Complete changes lifecycle, not human acceptance or verification. Unlink removes only the association. Related/implements Project relationships convey neither hierarchy nor inherited access. On a concurrent edit, retain your draft, load the latest version, reconcile and save explicitly.
