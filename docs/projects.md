# Organize work with Projects

Projects organize a shared initiative around objectives, deliverables and reviews. A Project is optional: existing PR, document, Draft, Form, Research and Live Preview reviews remain independently usable. A Tracker is also optional. Linking the same Resource to several Projects preserves its original identity, revisions and app-native threads.

## Create and resume

1. Select your personal or team workspace and open **Projects** under Work at `/workspaces/{workspaceId}/projects`.
2. Choose **New project**, select its initial workspace, and enter a name, purpose, objectives and context.
3. Choose workspace visibility (the team default) or participants-only visibility.
4. Open Overview to read the initiative, Work to link deliverables, Reviews to open review Resources, Activity for changes, and Settings for access/lifecycle.

Use collection search, lifecycle and visibility filters, and explicit next-page loading. Recently changed orders shared changes; it does not claim a personal visit. Links preserve selected tab and context.

## Access and lifecycle

Managers govern participants and lifecycle; contributors maintain authorized work; reviewers read/comment within their existing rights. Bring people into the workspace through Members before adding participation. Agents use durable registered Agent identities and workspace grants; they cannot govern human reviewer eligibility. Workspace membership, Project participation, Resource rights and provider authorization remain separate. An inaccessible linked Resource is omitted, including its title/count/revision; linking grants no source access.

Archive preserves linked Resources and blocks ordinary edits; authorized managers can restore. Complete changes lifecycle, not human acceptance or verification. Unlink removes only the association. Related/implements Project relationships convey neither hierarchy nor inherited access. On a concurrent edit, retain your draft, load the latest version, reconcile and save explicitly.
