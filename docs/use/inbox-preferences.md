Open Inbox preferences from your account controls to choose which attention events should notify you. Keep the feed as the authoritative list of active requests: a notification is a reminder, not a task receipt or approval.

Opening or scrolling through a card does not mark it read. Use the card's explicit action when you have replied, completed the request, or dismissed it. Completed and recipient-dismissed items appear in History. Sender and reason lines explain why an item is in your feed.

If an item refers to a Project or review you cannot open, request access to the underlying Resource; workspace membership, agent grants, and provider access are separate checks. Technical identifiers are available in the item's Technical details dialog.
